Acme Cloud Corp - Customer Policy & SLA Summary (2026)

1. Uptime SLA:
- Starter & Growth plans: 99.5% monthly uptime guarantee.
- Business plan: 99.95% monthly uptime guarantee with 24/7 priority support.

2. Support Response Times:
- Critical outage (P1): 30 minutes for Business, 4 hours for Growth, 24 hours for Starter.
- General inquiries: 1 business day across all paid plans.

3. Refund Policy:
- New annual subscriptions qualify for a full refund within 14 days of purchase.
- Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled anytime and remain active until the end of the billing cycle.

4. Data Security:
- All customer documents are encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).
- Enterprise customers can request full data deletion within 7 business days.
